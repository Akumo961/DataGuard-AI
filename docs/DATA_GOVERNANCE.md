# Data Governance

Maintain an inventory of data sources, owners, purposes, classifications, locations, recipients, retention periods and processing activities. Each organization should define its approved taxonomy and risk appetite.

DataGuard findings should retain detector provenance and confidence while minimizing raw personal information. Human reviewers validate high-impact findings and false positives.

Governance evidence should be versioned and tied to the applicable framework version and organizational policy. Legal applicability remains an organizational/legal determination.
