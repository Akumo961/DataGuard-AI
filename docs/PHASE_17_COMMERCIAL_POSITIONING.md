# Phase 17 — Commercial / Procurement Package

## Delivered

- Enterprise procurement package.
- Starter / Enterprise / Government offering structure.
- Separate implementation and professional-services model.
- Lifecycle support and maintenance model.
- Procurement evaluation criteria.
- Hypothetical ~$9M multi-year government program scenario.
- Explicit commercial and procurement disclaimers.

## Positioning principle

The product is positioned around measurable capabilities and implementation services. No claim is made that the software itself has a $9M value, that a government contract exists, or that government approval/certification has been obtained.

## Evidence required before actual procurement

- Production security assessment and penetration testing.
- Independent privacy/compliance review.
- Reproducible ML validation.
- Target-environment deployment validation.
- Accessibility assessment.
- Operational references and pilot outcomes.
- Contractual SLA, support and continuity commitments.
- Verified data-residency and sovereignty architecture.
