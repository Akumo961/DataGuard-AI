# Phase 6 — Risk Engine

## Repository evidence

The `dataguard/risk/` package provides a typed risk-engine boundary and deterministic privacy risk scoring implementation with explainable contributing factors. Automated tests cover deterministic scoring behavior.

## Scope

Risk assessment combines detected information characteristics and contextual factors rather than presenting an opaque legal decision. The engine is intended as decision support.

## Limitation

Risk scores are not legal conclusions and require organizational policy and qualified privacy/legal review. Production calibration requires representative validation data and operational feedback.
