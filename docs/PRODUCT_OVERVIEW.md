# DataGuard Québec — Product Overview

DataGuard Québec is an AI-assisted platform architecture for personal-information discovery, sensitivity classification, privacy risk assessment, governance workflows and evidence management.

## Positioning

The platform is designed as decision support for privacy, security and data-governance teams. It does not automatically establish legal compliance, provide legal advice, or confer certification.

## Core capabilities

- Document and data discovery
- Multi-layer PII detection
- Explainable risk scoring
- Configurable privacy controls
- PIA workflow
- Audit and evidence management
- Human review
- Enterprise API and web interface

## Enterprise boundary

Production deployments require organization-specific identity, integrations, retention, key management, monitoring, legal control mapping and operational procedures.
