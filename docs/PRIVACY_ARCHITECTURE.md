# Privacy Architecture

DataGuard applies privacy-by-design through minimization, tenant-scoped authorization, configurable retention, redacted responses, provenance, human review and auditable governance workflows.

Raw sensitive documents should be retained only when necessary and authorized. Prefer metadata, extracted findings and redacted evidence where the use case permits. Deletion must be policy-driven and verified by the target persistence/object-storage implementation.

Purpose, recipients, storage, retention, safeguards and risks are captured by the PIA model. Privacy incident handling is governed by the organization's approved procedures.
