# Incident Response

## Severity

- SEV-1: suspected active sensitive-data compromise, tenant isolation failure or material service compromise.
- SEV-2: significant security/privacy degradation without confirmed material compromise.
- SEV-3: contained defect or low-impact security event.

## Response

1. Detect and preserve evidence.
2. Assign incident commander and privacy/security leads.
3. Contain affected accounts, tokens, workloads or network paths.
4. Determine affected tenants/data and preserve relevant audit records.
5. Eradicate root cause and rotate compromised credentials.
6. Recover from known-good artifacts/backups.
7. Validate security controls and tenant isolation.
8. Conduct post-incident review and corrective actions.
9. Apply notification/communication procedures required by the organization's legal and privacy governance.

Do not place sensitive incident details into public issue trackers. Legal/privacy notification decisions require the organization's authorized personnel.
