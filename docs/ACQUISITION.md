# DataGuard Acquisition Evidence Framework

## Strategic thesis

DataGuard's potential strategic value is the combination of sensitive-data discovery, explainable classification and risk, regulatory mapping, governance workflow, and auditable evidence in one tenant-aware platform.

## Technology moat candidates

- Tenant-safe sensitive-data analysis pipeline.
- Provider-neutral connector boundary with a real S3-compatible adapter.
- Explainable classification/risk provenance with organization-scoped policy storage.
- First-class findings and governance workflow with lifecycle evidence.
- Tamper-evident audit chain with request provenance.
- Configurable regulatory and classification control models.

These are **potential** moats. They are not asserted as proprietary intellectual property until ownership, originality, licensing and competitive analysis are documented.

## Evidence required for a $9M–$15M strategic case

### Product
- Production reliability evidence.
- Enterprise integrations with least-privilege acceptance tests.
- Measured customer outcomes.
- Executable asynchronous processing evidence at enterprise scale.

### Security
- Independent penetration test.
- Vulnerability-management history.
- SBOM and supply-chain controls.
- Demonstrated tenant-isolation tests across read/create/update/delete/inference.
- OIDC provider lifecycle, role mapping, key rotation and session-revocation acceptance tests.
- Malware scanning and quarantine evidence in a production-like environment.

### AI/data quality
- Reproducible synthetic benchmark corpus and evaluator are present in `benchmarks/`.
- Precision/recall/F1 and false-positive analysis must be captured from a completed benchmark run; no score is claimed merely because the evaluator exists.
- Versioned model/detector provenance.

### Operations
- Reproducible backup/restore exercise is present; measured RPO/RTO still requires production-like evidence.
- Kubernetes rollout/rollback manifests are present; upgrade/rollback execution still requires evidence.
- Production observability must include distributed traces and alert delivery evidence, not only process-local counters.
- Incident-response exercises remain required.

### Commercial / legal
- Recurring revenue.
- Customer retention and expansion.
- Referenceable regulated customers.
- Contractual/IP ownership clarity.
- Independent security and legal due diligence.

A $9M–$15M acquisition price is not guaranteed by the software and must be supported by independent business, legal, security and commercial evidence.
